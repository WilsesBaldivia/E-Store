COLLEGE REGULAR UNIFORM - DEPARTMENT NAME GUIDE
================================================

The five College regular-uniform photos are connected to the School Uniform
card. A Department selector appears only after College is selected.

Current mapping:

01  UNI-COL-REG  uniform-college-department-01.jpg        Department 01 (Rename Me)
02  UNI-COL-02   uniform-college-department-02-clean.png  Department 02 (Rename Me)
03  UNI-COL-03   uniform-college-department-03-clean.png  Department 03 (Rename Me)
04  UNI-COL-04   uniform-college-department-04.jpg        Department 04 (Rename Me)
05  UNI-COL-05   uniform-college-department-05.jpg        Department 05 (Rename Me)

HOW TO CHANGE A DEPARTMENT NAME
-------------------------------

1. Open e_storedb.sql.
2. Search for the product SKU, for example UNI-COL-02.
3. Change only the product name after "College School Uniform -".
   Example:
   College School Uniform - Department 02 (Rename Me)
   becomes:
   College School Uniform - Nursing
4. Import e_storedb.sql again in phpMyAdmin. The existing product will update
   without creating a duplicate.

The PHP 720.00 price and stock of 20 per size are starter values that the
administrator can update later.
