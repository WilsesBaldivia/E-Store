Place the official product photos in this folder.

Automatic uniform filenames:
- uniform-elementary
- uniform-jhs-clean
- uniform-shs-clean
- active College regular uniforms: departments 01, 02, 03, and 05
- archived College regular uniform: department 04 (not shown in the store)
- active Type B photos: typeb-department-02, 03, 06, 08, and 09
- archived Type B photos: 01, 04, 05, 07, and 10 (not shown in the store)
- cleaned catalog versions: 02-clean, 03-clean, 06-clean, 08-clean, and 09-clean
- pe-elementary
- pe-jhs
- pe-shs
- pe-college

Supported file types: .jpg, .jpeg, .png, and .webp
Example: uniform-jhs.png or uniform-jhs.jpg

When one of these files exists, the student catalog automatically displays it
after the matching academic level is selected.

For the Type B department-name guide, open TYPE_B_DEPARTMENTS.txt.
For the College regular-uniform guide, open COLLEGE_UNIFORM_DEPARTMENTS.txt.
